Try to defend the flag! To protect the flag from enemy tanks, you have your tank at your disposal. But be careful! Your tank will be destroyed by one shot from an enemy tank. Sometimes the enemies will also be joined by aircraft, which will instantly destroy you when they fly over you. There will also be the occasional bomb on the ground. You have to get to it and defuse it as soon as possible, otherwise it will blow everything up.

Control your tank with the "W" "A" "S" "D" keys and shoot with the "SPACE" key.

Now that you know that, let's fight!
